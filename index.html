GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
you go?

Here are a few details to help you get started.

What documents do I need to rent a car?+

Typically, renters need a valid driving licence and passport or ID. Minimum age and licence-holding requirements depend on the rental provider—confirm these before booking.

Can I pick up the car at the airport?+

Airport pick-up can be requested in the booking form. It is subject to confirmation and may involve an additional fee.

Is insurance included in the daily price?+

Insurance coverage and any excess depend on the rental agreement. Ask for the full terms, deposit amount, mileage limits, and cross-border rules before paying.

How do I confirm my booking?+

Submit the request form and contact the rental team using the details listed on this website. A booking is not confirmed until the provider replies and agrees to the terms.
